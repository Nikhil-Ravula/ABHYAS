Contributor Guide

Learn how to share study resources with the ABHYAS community

Guide 1

How to Upload PYQs (Previous Year Papers)

  • Go to the Upload PYQ page from your Home dashboard.
  • Enter the Subject name (e.g. Web Technologies) and select the exam Year.
  • Select the academic Regulation (e.g. R22) and the Exam Type (Regular, Supply, or Mid).
  • Select the relevant engineering Branches and add relevant Hashtags (separated by commas) to help students search easily.
  • Attach the question paper file (PDF, Doc, or Image format).
  • Attribution Option: Choose if you want to display your name on the paper card.
    If you check "Click this to show that the above PYQ is uploaded by you", other users will see your username/name. Otherwise, the uploader defaults to Nikhil Ravula (Site Owner).
Guide 2

How to Upload IQs (Important Questions)

  • Go to the Upload IQ page from your Home dashboard.
  • Select the Subject, Regulation, Unit (1 to 5), and Question Type (Short Answer or Long Answer).
  • You will see 10 question fields by default. Fill in the question texts.
  • If a question has an image or diagram, you can optionally attach an image or PDF to individual questions. You can also submit a question with just a file/image and no text!
  • Need to add more? Click the Add More Questions button to insert additional slots.
Guide 3

Review & Approval Lifecycle

  • Once submitted, your resources will go into a Pending review state.
  • Admins will review the content to verify accuracy, quality, and check for duplicates.
  • If accepted, the paper or important questions will go live on the platform instantly, attributed to your chosen name preference!
